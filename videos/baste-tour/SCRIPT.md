# Baste tour — narration

Voice: Cartesia sonic-3.6, Theo (scripts/cartesia.mjs), fitted to each scene with --keep-timing.
One line per scene; each scene holds until its line ends plus a short breath.

| # | id | scene | line |
|---|----|-------|------|
| 1 | why | Title over pattern paper | Most interfaces are cut for “users aged twenty-five to thirty-four.” Nobody in that bracket has taste. A person does. |
| 2 | measure | Three portraits, their influences | Baste starts with one person: the films they rewatch, the music they work to, the rooms they feel at home in, and the things they can’t stand in an app. |
| 3 | fitting | The same screen re-cut for Moss, Ren and Ada | Then it fits an interface to them. Same screen, three people. The layout, the type, the colour and the motion all change. |
| 4 | baste | The archive filling with real candidates | To find a direction, Baste tries dozens of loose versions, warm and cool, sparse and dense, and a vision judge looks at every one. |
| 5 | fit | Judge weights; the kept variants | It keeps the best in each region, not one winner. So you see the real range of options before anything is final. |
| 6 | cut | Finished suite: hero, icons, loop, tokens | What comes out is ready to use: images, vector icons, ambient loops, and design tokens for CSS, Tailwind or Panda. |
| 7 | talk | Conversation with Ren | And you can ask the persona why. They’ll tell you, in their own words, what they love and what would make a design feel like theirs. |
| 8 | yours | Studio, command, end card | Bring your own people. Measure them in the Studio, and cut something that fits. |
