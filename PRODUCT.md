# Baste

## Register

product

## Users

Designers and developers building interfaces for people with specific cultural influences. The Studio is a workroom for editing personas, fitting design tokens, collecting references and generating assets; the public site explains the method.

## Product Purpose

Turn films, music, spaces, tools and obsessions into usable, culturally specific UI assets. Explore diverse candidates and retain a useful archive, rather than collapsing the process into a single answer.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, attentive, particular. The existing house style is a tailor's atelier: pattern paper, ink, thread and chalk. Keep its quiet framing so a persona's own palette remains legible.

## Anti-references

Generic demographic personas, interchangeable AI asset galleries, decorative controls that obscure the fitting process, and account or payment states that imply access before it is verified.

## Design Principles

- Cultural influences are the measurements; demographics alone do not determine a fitting.
- Preserve diverse options and show useful production states.
- Reuse the established atelier components and tokens.
- Keep local work, provider credentials and hosted account state understandable.
- Show verified availability, failures and next actions in the flow where they matter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the existing semantic forms, keyboard focus, skip links, readable text contrast and reduced-motion alternatives. Keep controls usable on narrow screens with accessible names when labels collapse.

This context records the existing AGENT_REFERENCE.md, Panda tokens and Studio component conventions; it does not introduce a new visual direction.
